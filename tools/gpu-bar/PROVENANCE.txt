dma/ (arm G; current Sophia master, revision 10) is the same peer's fresh
workload with only the files wire and a DMA-BUF source. It shares journal.rs
from historical/ by #[path] and the producer from common/. SDK:
  https://github.com/sophia-org/sophia-desktop-sdk-rs
  0706ea2c78cd16ba59ed209620354f30a2c78906 (Lom's pin; Sophia master vendors the same)
Image bounds come from ContentLimits as in Lom. Metadata follows the r10 kind-2
rules: declared_bytes = width*height*4, stride 0; the plane offset and stride
go beside the fd. The SDK checks the descriptor size against
max_dma_source_bytes. Upload acceptance requires ResourceStatus Accepted and a
Settled offer with disposition 2 (captured); Ambiguous or Expired offers keep
waiting until the deadline, as in Lom. The export is freed only after that
settlement, or after a refusal that returned the descriptor. On any other
ending (no descriptor back, status other than Accepted, Refused, Unknown or
timeout) the frame is retained until process exit.

Both locks were seeded from Lom ae28569's Cargo.lock so the GPU stack matches
Lom's tested versions. Build:
  cargo-slot niltempus cargo build --release --offline --locked --manifest-path tools/gpu-bar/<historical|dma>/Cargo.toml
